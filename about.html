About Us

This webpage was built to serve as a character guide / introduction to the characters of Spongebob Square Pants. On this page, you will find different buttons above that will take you to different areas including the homepage, character details page, this page (about us), and a button to request a new character. The character request button will take you to a new page including a form for you to fill out requesting a character be added with their backstory and information. I tried to keep everything here pretty simple and easy to read so I hope you enjoy it!
