SpongeBob SquarePants

About SpongeBob

SpongeBob is an upbeat sea sponge who lives in a pineapple under the sea in a town called Bikini Bottom. In this town, he works as a frycook at the Krusty Krab resteraunt. He is enrolled as Mrs. Puffs boating school, and has yet to pass the class because he cant keep the boat from crashing. At home, he has a pet snail named Gary who he loves very much. Spongebobs residence is located right beside of Squidward and one house over from his best friend Patricks. He is considered by everyone in town to be upbeat and pretty annoying most of the time leading to the city taking a "break from Spongebob" day. In his free time, he loves to go jelly fishing and engage in some karate training with his other friend Sandy Cheeks.
